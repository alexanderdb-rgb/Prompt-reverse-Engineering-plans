Cleanse Scope: What the Cleanse Worker Mutates

Record Fields
email PRESERVED
best_contact_email PRESERVED
timestamp PRESERVED
status MUTATED (set to CLEANSED_PASSED or QUARANTINE_FAILED)
cleansed_at MUTATED (timestamp set)
cleansing_log MUTATED (entry appended)
application_data.* CLEANSED (recursive regex + Gemini)
The cleanse worker NEVER touches email, best_contact_email, or timestamp.
These fields are set once by the ingest pipeline and remain immutable.
Preserved (immutable)
Mutated (status tracking)
Cleansed (data hygiene)
